What We Offer

Complete Marine Service Portfolio

Modern Power Marine Services delivers end-to-end marine solutions from routine maintenance to complex overhauls. Our integrated service capability means clients can rely on a single trusted partner for all their marine needs.

Mechanical Repair

Engine overhaul, pump repair, gearbox servicing, and complete mechanical system maintenance for marine vessels.

Hull Repair

Steel and aluminum hull fabrication, welding, structural repairs, and corrosion treatment for all vessel types.

Vessel Maintenance

Scheduled and emergency maintenance programs ensuring optimal performance and regulatory compliance.

Propulsion Systems

Shaft alignment, propeller repair and replacement, gearbox overhaul, and thruster maintenance.

Steel Fabrication

Custom steel structures, platforms, marine equipment fabrication, and on-site welding services.

Surface Treatment

Abrasive blasting, protective coating application, and anti-fouling systems for marine environments.

Electrical Works

Marine electrical installation, automation systems, control panel design, and troubleshooting.

Piping Systems

Hydraulic, pneumatic, and process piping design, fabrication, installation, and maintenance.

Naval Architecture

Vessel design, stability analysis, modification engineering, and classification society liaison.

Equipment Supply

Supply of marine spare parts, tools, machinery, and specialized equipment from global manufacturers.

Safety Equipment

Life-saving appliances, firefighting systems, personal protective equipment, and safety compliance services.

Navigation Systems

Installation, calibration, and maintenance of navigation, communication, and electronic systems.

Underwater Services

Hull cleaning, underwater inspection, propeller polishing, and minor underwater repairs by certified divers.

Logistics Support

Warehousing, procurement, supply chain management, and just-in-time delivery for marine operations.

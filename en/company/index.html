Company

A Saudi marine services company based in Jeddah, serving government and private-sector clients on the Red Sea and Arabian Gulf coasts.

About MPMS

Modern Power Marine Services Co. (MPMS) is a Saudi limited liability company headquartered in Jeddah. It began in 2000 as an establishment supplying ship spare parts and providing ship maintenance and operation services, and in 2012 expanded into a marine services company.

Today MPMS maintains and repairs vessels of all sizes — drydocking, mechanical, electrical and hull work, engines and generators from 10 kW to 3,000 kW — and builds, maintains and equips marine facilities, berths and fenders. It also supplies firefighting, desalination and air-conditioning systems, marine equipment and spare parts, and skilled technical manpower.

The company serves government and private-sector clients on the Red Sea and Arabian Gulf coasts, including the Royal Saudi Naval Forces, the Border Guard, the Saline Water Conversion Corporation, Saudi Aramco, SAMREF, Ma'aden and KAUST, and represents manufacturers such as SCHOTTEL, HI-FOG, Yantai TaiHong, Sunhelm and Hongruntong in the Kingdom.

Vision, mission and objective

Vision
To be the leading Saudi company in managing, operating, docking and maintaining vessels on the Kingdom's eastern and western coasts, and to increase investment in our region in line with our values and the Kingdom's Vision 2030.
Mission
To be an integral part of the Saudi business community, particularly in the maritime sector.
Objective
To provide marine services and maintenance for local and international vessels to high quality standards, while building the name and reputation of a Saudi company in the national and international maritime community.

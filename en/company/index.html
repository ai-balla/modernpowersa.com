Company

A Saudi marine services company based in Jeddah, serving government and private-sector clients across the Kingdom.

History

  1. 2000

    MPMS began in Jeddah as an establishment supplying ship spare parts and providing ship maintenance and operation services.

  2. 2012

    The business expanded into Modern Power Marine Services Co., adding marine works, systems supply and skilled technical manpower to its maintenance and spare-parts activities.

Vision, mission and objective

Vision
To be the leading Saudi company in managing, operating, docking and maintaining vessels on the Kingdom's eastern and western coasts, and to increase investment in our region in line with our values and the Kingdom's Vision 2030.
Mission
To be an integral part of the Saudi business community, particularly in the maritime sector.
Objective
To provide marine services and maintenance for local and international vessels to high quality standards, while building the name and reputation of a Saudi company in the national and international maritime community.

Eng. Mohamed Jazzar

Chairman and Founder

A marine engineer with more than 40 years in the maritime industry, covering ship maintenance and marine operations: mechanical, electrical, refrigeration and air conditioning, painting, carpentry, marine fittings, and the supply and installation of water desalination stations.

Career

  1. Graduated in 1978 and began his career with the Saudi Ports Authority as Director of Maritime Administration at Jeddah Islamic Port

  2. Moved to the private sector in 1992 as General Manager of Yanbu Commercial Port

  3. Chairman of the Board and General Manager of King Fahd Shipyard in Dammam for four years

  4. General Manager of King Fahd Shipyard at Jeddah Islamic Port for eleven years

Experience and achievements

  • Oversaw the repair of the ship Makkah after a major incident
  • Led the lifting and transport of the capsized boat Zorak 54 from Tair Island to King Faisal Naval Base in Jeddah
  • Direct contracts with the Royal Saudi Naval Forces for the maintenance, operation, supply and installation of marine equipment, including repair of fast-boat control rooms and upgrades of vessel water systems

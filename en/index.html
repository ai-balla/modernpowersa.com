What we do

Nine service lines, from a single repair to a full docking period. Each page lists the typical scope and what you receive.

  • Vessel Maintenance and Repair

    Planned and corrective maintenance for ships, boats and tugs, covering mechanical, electrical and hull work, including docking periods.

  • Hull, Steelwork and Coatings

    Steel renewal, welding, fabrication, surface preparation and painting for hulls and marine structures.

  • Engines, Generators and Propulsion

    Inspection, repair and maintenance of marine diesel engines, generators, gearboxes and transmissions.

  • Berths, Fenders and Marine Facilities

    Maintenance and equipment for fixed and floating berths, marine fenders, navigation marks and berth equipment.

  • Marine Firefighting and Safety Systems

    Supply, installation and maintenance of firefighting systems and safety equipment for vessels and marine facilities.

  • Water Desalination Systems

    Supply, installation and maintenance of seawater desalination systems for vessels and marine facilities.

  • Air Conditioning and Ventilation

    Supply, installation and maintenance of shipboard and facility air conditioning, ventilation and ductwork.

  • Equipment and Spare Parts Supply

    Sourcing of marine and industrial equipment and spare parts through manufacturers and suppliers.

  • Skilled Technical Manpower

    Engineers, technicians and tradespeople provided under contract to support maintenance work.

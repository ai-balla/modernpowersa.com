Water Desalination Systems

MPMS supplies, installs and maintains seawater desalination systems on vessels and at marine facilities.

Typical scope

  • Supply of seawater desalination units
  • Installation on board vessels and at marine facilities
  • Maintenance and repair of desalination systems

What you receive

  1. Review of the water demand and installation constraints
  2. Itemised quotation with unit specifications
  3. Completion and handover on the acceptance basis set in the contract

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS teams, with equipment and systems sourced from manufacturers.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

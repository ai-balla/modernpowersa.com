Berths, Fenders and Marine Facilities

MPMS maintains marine facilities and their equipment, and supplies and installs fenders and navigation marks. Fenders and specialist equipment are sourced from manufacturers and installed by MPMS teams.

Typical scope

  • Maintenance of marine facilities and berth equipment
  • Construction and maintenance of fixed and floating berths
  • Manufacture, supply and installation of marine fenders
  • Supply, installation and maintenance of marine navigation marks
  • Replacement and maintenance of fuel unloading arms at marine berths
  • Marine survey and underwater works
  • Rubber and Azobé hardwood fenders, dock airbags, marine hoses and mooring equipment from the manufacturers we represent

What you receive

  1. Site inspection and a proposed scope of work
  2. Itemised quotation, including equipment specifications where supply is involved
  3. Completion and handover on the acceptance basis set in the contract

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS teams, with equipment and systems sourced from manufacturers.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

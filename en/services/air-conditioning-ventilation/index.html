Air Conditioning and Ventilation

MPMS supplies, installs and maintains air conditioning and ventilation systems on vessels and in shore facilities, including ductwork cleaning and control-panel maintenance.

Typical scope

  • Ship air-conditioning units
  • Ducted, chiller and packaged air-conditioning systems
  • Supply, installation and cleaning of ventilation ducts
  • Preventive and corrective maintenance of air-conditioning control panels

What you receive

  1. Site or on-board survey and a proposed scope of work
  2. Itemised quotation with equipment specifications
  3. Completion and handover on the acceptance basis set in the contract

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS engineers, technicians and trades.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

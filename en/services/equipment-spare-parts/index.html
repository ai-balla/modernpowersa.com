Services

Equipment and Spare Parts Supply

MPMS sources marine and industrial equipment and spare parts through its network of manufacturers and suppliers, and can install and commission supplied equipment where the scope requires it.

Typical scope

  • Marine cranes, including barge-mounted cranes, and industrial cranes
  • Diesel and electric forklifts and warehouse handling equipment
  • Fuel unloading arms for marine berths
  • Generators, and engine and generator spare parts
  • Navigation equipment and marine safety equipment
  • Boats and marine craft through boat-builder partners
  • Crane and equipment rental
  • Navigation radar supply and installation

What you receive

  1. Technical and commercial offer against the purchaser's specification
  2. Delivery to site under the agreed terms
  3. Installation and commissioning where included in the scope

How it is delivered

Supplied through manufacturers and suppliers; installation by MPMS where included in the scope.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

Skilled Technical Manpower

Under its contractual manpower programme, MPMS provides skilled personnel to support clients' maintenance operations.

Typical scope

  • Engineers and technicians in mechanical and electrical disciplines
  • Welders and fabricators
  • Divers
  • General workers

What you receive

  1. Proposed staffing with roles and quantities
  2. Rates and mobilisation terms
  3. Deployment for the agreed duration

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS engineers, technicians and trades.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

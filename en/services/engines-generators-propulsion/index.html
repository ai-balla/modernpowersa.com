Engines, Generators and Propulsion

MPMS maintains and repairs marine engines and generators of different sizes, on board vessels and at industrial sites, and supplies the related spare parts.

Typical scope

  • Inspection and repair of marine and industrial generators
  • Maintenance of main and auxiliary diesel engines
  • Gearbox and transmission repairs
  • Supply of engine and generator spare parts
  • Engine and generator overhauls from 10 kW to 3,000 kW
  • Diesel injector repair
  • SCHOTTEL propulsion systems, as authorised agent

What you receive

  1. Inspection findings and a list of required parts
  2. Itemised quotation for labour and parts
  3. Completion and handover on the acceptance basis set in the contract

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS engineers, technicians and trades.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

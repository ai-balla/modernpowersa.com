Services

Marine Firefighting and Safety Systems

MPMS supplies and installs firefighting systems for vessels and marine facilities, working with system manufacturers, and supplies marine safety equipment for boats and ships.

Typical scope

  • Supply and installation of firefighting systems for vessels and marine facilities
  • Maintenance of installed firefighting systems
  • Supply of marine safety equipment for boats and ships
  • HI-FOG high-pressure water-mist systems, as authorised agent

What you receive

  1. Review of the vessel or facility requirement
  2. Itemised quotation with system and equipment specifications
  3. Completion and handover on the acceptance basis set in the contract

How it is delivered

Carried out by MPMS teams, with equipment and systems sourced from manufacturers.

Request a quotation

Send the specification, drawings or a description of the work, with the vessel or site location. If you are preparing a tender, include the reference number and closing date.

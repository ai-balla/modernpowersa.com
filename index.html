About Us

Leading Marine Services
in Saudi Arabia

Modern Power Marine Services (MPMS) was established in 2000 in Jeddah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. As a Class II Marine Contractor, we deliver comprehensive ship repair, marine works, vessel maintenance, and equipment supply services across the Kingdom.

With over two decades of experience, we proudly serve القوات البحرية الملكية السعودية, المؤسسة العامة لتحلية المياه المالحة, المديرية العامة لحرس الحدود, and major industrial clients with unwavering commitment to quality and safety.

Our Story

Building Marine Excellence Since Day One

Modern Power Marine Services (MPMS) was founded with a clear mission: to provide world-class marine services that meet the growing demands of Saudi Arabia's maritime industry. From our headquarters in Jeddah, we have expanded our operations to serve clients across the Kingdom's Eastern and Western coasts.

Today, MPMS is a trusted partner to القوات البحرية الملكية السعودية, المؤسسة العامة لتحلية المياه المالحة, المديرية العامة لحرس الحدود, and major industrial clients. Our team of certified marine engineers, naval architects, and skilled technicians brings decades of combined experience to every project.

  • ISO Certified across multiple standards
  • Class II Marine Contractor certification
  • Operations on both coasts of Saudi Arabia
  • Dedicated team of 120+ professionals
